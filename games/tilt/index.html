How to play: tilt the board and the ball slides on the ice until it hits a rock or the edge. Get it to stop on the star; sliding over it doesn't count. You can fail: you get twice the minimum number of tilts. Undo and restart are free, but they don't give tilts back. Swipe the board, use the arrows, or press arrow keys / WASD. Z to undo.
