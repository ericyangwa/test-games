How to play: recreate the target break exactly. There is one correct answer: the right spots, the right forces, in the right order. Strikes snap to a grid, and the same strike always breaks the same way. Order matters: new cracks stop when they hit old ones. You can fail: you get twice the minimum number of hits. Undo and new glass are free, but they don't give swings back. Reach 100% before you run out, or the glass wins. Keys: 1/2/3 to pick force, Z to undo.
