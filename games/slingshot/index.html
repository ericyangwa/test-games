    How to play: launch the probe from the pad and get it into the beacon. Planets pull on it, and the straight shot is always blocked, so you have to bend it around them. Every launch starts from the pad, and the same angle and power always fly the same path. Your old shots stay on screen. You can fail: you get 6 launches. Fewest launches wins. Drag on the space to aim (farther = more power), or use the sliders. Keys: ←/→ angle, ↑/↓ power, Space to launch.
