How to play: listen to a sentence and name the language. At first you only hear the start of it. Every miss or skip lets you hear more, and after 3 misses you get written clues. Misses still help: each wrong guess shows how far its homeland is from the answer's, which way to go, and whether it's in the same branch or family. You can fail: 6 guesses. The fewer you need, the better. Easy languages early in the week, hard ones at the weekend.
