    Prototype tools
    How to play: make one straight cut through the pin ● that splits the object into two halves of equal size. Exactly one angle does it. Drag on the board to swing the knife around the pin, fine-tune with ⟲ ⟳, then press Cut. Each cut tells you how it split. Weight days: some days the object is made of materials with different weights, shown under the board, and the halves must weigh the same. You can fail: you get 4 cuts. Land within half a point of 50/50 to win. Fewest cuts wins. Easy objects early in the week, holes and scattered pieces midweek, weights at the weekend. Keys: ←/→ rotate (hold Shift for big steps), Enter to cut.
