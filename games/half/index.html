The island of
    Prototype tools
    How to play: draw one straight border through the capital ★ that splits the island into two halves of equal area. Exactly one angle does it. Drag on the map to swing the line around the capital, fine-tune with ⟲ ⟳, then press Cut. Each cut tells you how the land was split. You can fail: you get 4 cuts. Land within half a point of 50/50 to win. Fewest cuts wins. Keys: ←/→ rotate (hold Shift for big steps), Enter to cut.
