How to play: a wreck lies on one square of open water. Tap a water square to ping it. The ping tells you how many squares away the wreck is, measured in a straight line and rounded to the nearest whole square. Ping the wreck's own square (range 0) to find it. You can't ping land, and the wreck is never under it. You can fail: you get 6 pings. Fewest pings wins.
