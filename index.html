Daily Games Lab

Prototypes for playtesting. Each game has a new puzzle every day, one correct answer, and a score you can compare.

Language · Prototype

Polyglot

Hear a sentence and name the language. You hear more after every miss, and each wrong guess tells you how far off you are.

 Logic · Prototype

Tilt

Tilt an icy board and the ball slides until it hits a rock. Make it stop on the star in as few tilts as you can.

 Physics · Prototype

Slingshot

Space golf. Launch a probe past planets whose gravity bends its path, and get it into the beacon in the fewest launches.

 Deduction · Prototype

Sonar

A wreck is hidden somewhere in the sea. Each ping tells you how far away it is. Triangulate it in the fewest pings.

 Geometry · Prototype

Half

Draw one straight border through the capital that splits the island into two equal halves.

 Physics · Prototype

Fracture

Strike a pane of glass to recreate the target crack pattern exactly. Spot, force and order all matter.
